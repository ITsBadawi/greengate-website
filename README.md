# GREEN GATE | شركة البوابة الخضراء للتجارة العامة والخدمات العامة ذ.م.م

> **Ultra-Luxury Corporate Website Redesign ($10,000 Tier Design)**  
> Built with **GSAP 3.12 (GreenSock Animation Platform)**, **Lenis Smooth Scroll**, and **Bilingual (Arabic / English)** support.

---

## 🌟 Overview (نظرة عامة)

A next-generation corporate web platform designed for **Green Gate for General Trade & General Services LLC** (Baghdad, Iraq). The site highlights international trading networks, strategic warehousing complexes across Iraq and Egypt, executive leadership, and global trade corridors (China, Turkey, Europe, SE Asia).

---

## ✨ Key Features (أبرز المميزات)

- 💎 **Ultra-Luxury Aesthetic**: Deep obsidian & dark emerald glassmorphism styling (`#05080A`, `#10B981`, `#34D399`).
- ⚡ **GSAP 3.12 & ScrollTrigger**: Cinematic entrance timelines, staggered reveals, counter animations, and parallax effects.
- 🌊 **Lenis Momentum Smooth Scrolling**: 60fps butter-smooth momentum physics synchronized with GSAP ticker.
- 🌐 **Instant Bilingual Support (AR / EN)**: Full real-time switching between Arabic (RTL) and English (LTR) without page reloads.
- 🗺️ **Interactive Global Trade Map**: Custom SVG trade lines connecting Guangzhou, Istanbul, Bangkok/KL, and Europe directly to Baghdad HQ and Cairo plant.
- 🏭 **Interactive Facilities & Warehouses Showcase**: Tabbed dynamic viewer for Egypt Factory and Baghdad warehouses (Al-Jamia & Al-Shurja 1 & 2) with technical specifications and fullscreen lightbox zoom.
- 🎬 **Activities & Exhibitions Showcase**: Video player for Nelly Clothing Exhibition (Egypt), Canton Fair 2026 (China), Company Anniversary, and Art Workshop.
- 👥 **Executive Board 3D Tilt Cards**: Executive profiles with direct social links.
- 📩 **Working Contact & RFQ Suite**: Integrated with EmailJS and 1-tap WhatsApp consultation.

## 🚀 Quick Start (التشغيل المحلي)

```bash

# Node.js
npx serve .
```
